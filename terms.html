1. Scope

auto_shorts_bot is a personal utility for the operator’s authorized YouTube channel. It assists with public video research, original fictional script generation, media assembly, uploading, scheduling and local channel reporting. These informational pages do not offer a public account or paid subscription.

2. Authorization and control

Connect only a channel you own or are authorized to manage. Grant access through Google’s OAuth flow and keep credentials private. Enabling unattended publication authorizes uploads and scheduled publication according to the configured workflow. The operator remains responsible for selecting the destination channel, checking initial output and controlling the workflow.

Generated stories are fictional and may use synthetic narration. Stock images are atmospheric illustrations rather than evidence of an event. You must check the suitability, accuracy, rights and required attribution of output before operating the publishing workflow.
